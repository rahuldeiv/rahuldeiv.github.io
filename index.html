01 / INTRODUCTION

A bit about me.

How do we make intelligent systems more secure?

I’m a master’s student in Cybersecurity & AI at Radboud University in Nijmegen, the Netherlands, and a research intern with its Digital Security Group (DiS). My interests lie at the intersection of security and machine learning: AI-driven threats, the vulnerabilities of large language models, and adversarial robustness.

My research explores indirect prompt injection in mobile AI agents and the robustness of LLM watermarking against adversarial attacks. Before moving into research, I spent four years at Deloitte Consulting USI developing and maintaining enterprise SAP systems.

Off the clock, I enjoy cooking, gaming on my PlayStation 5, and backpacking through new cities.

Security research & machine learning.

01
AGENTIC AI · SECURITYResearch project

Prompt injection in mobile AI agents.

Designed an attack taxonomy and benchmark across nine Android accessibility attack pathways for Mobilerun and Mobile-Use. Experiments achieved attack success rates of 82% on Gemma4 and 54% on Qwen3.6.

NLP · MODEL FINE-TUNINGKaggle project

Translating Akkadian with LLMs.

Built a dual-architecture translation pipeline with byte-level ByT5 and a 9B-parameter Tower+ model fine-tuned using PEFT/QLoRA. Developed memory-efficient inference across dual T4 GPUs using CPU offloading and BF16 mixed precision.
